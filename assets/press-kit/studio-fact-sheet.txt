ABOUT THE STUDIO
Founded in 2020, II Games Studio is an independent game development studio
dedicated to fast-paced gameplay and striking atmospheres. The studio makes
mechanics-driven platformers and atmospheric horror, primarily in Unity.

GAME SELECTION
CubeGame (Windows)
A 2D platformer with player-placed respawn flags and electrical platforms.
https://iigames.github.io/games/cubegame

No man's land (Browser, Windows; in development)
A PS1-style first-person game about the fear of World War I trench warfare.
https://iigames.github.io/games/nomansland

SCP 173 Simulator (Windows)
A role-reversal horror game built for GMTK Game Jam 2023.
https://iigames.github.io/games/scp173simulator

Slowly Strange (Windows)
An ordinary routine turns uneasy in this first-person horror game.
https://iigames.github.io/games/slowlystrange
