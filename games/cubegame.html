About the game

A mechanics-driven 2D platformer set in a world ruled by bosses. Take control of a cube with tools that change how you cross each level.

How it plays

Respawn flag: Place a flag to choose where you return after hitting lava. Without a flag, you lose health and return to the starting point.

Electrical platforms: Cross difficult sections at the cost of one battery charge. Buy more charges at the shop and decide where to use them.

Controls

Move: WASD
Jump: Space
Shoot: E
Interact: R
Place flag: F
Menu selection: Enter or left click
Menu navigation: Arrow keys

PlayStation controllers are supported. The in-game controls menu lists gamepad bindings. Play without DS4Windows for the recommended setup.
