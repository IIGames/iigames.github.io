A new home for II Games news

Game archives and development notes now have a place of their own on the II Games website.

1 min read

News now has its own space on the II Games Studio website. You can browse game releases, read development notes and find updates from the studio.

Start with the archive

The first posts revisit games already in the catalog, from CubeGame Alpha to No man’s land. Their dates follow the dates recorded on the game pages. They are archive entries, rather than new release announcements.

Reading and sharing

Each article has a reading-time estimate and a link you can copy. Categories separate game releases from development notes, and you can sort the archive from newest or oldest.

Have a game in mind? Browse the games, or read about II Games.
