CubeGame Alpha is the early version of CubeGame, developed and published by II Games. It combines platforming with puzzles.

## Keeping the early version

The alpha has its own page in the archive, separate from the later CubeGame. You can find its available platform links and trailer there.

- [Open CubeGame Alpha](/games/cubegame-alpha)
- [See the later CubeGame](/games/cubegame)

Both versions remain available in the catalog so you can choose which one to try.
